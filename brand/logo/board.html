What we heard

The brief

A home practice app for adults recovering language after a stroke. Often older, may use one hand, tires quickly. The logo should feel calm, hopeful and clinical but warm.

calmhopefulclinicalwarm

Success: readable at 16 px, works in one colour, feels steady rather than childlike, and avoids the brain, heart and pulse-line clichés of health apps.

RehabMind — identity concepts2
Concept A

Held R

An R with a break in its stem, where one dot holds the gap like a word coming back.

  • Stays a clear R from 16 px upward
  • Steady construction reads clinical, rounded ends and the warm dot read human
  • Links directly to the name, so it can stand alone as the app icon

Recommendation & next steps

Our recommendation

Held R is the chosen logo: the clearest mark at every size, and it links directly to the name.

  • Run a professional trademark search before any public pilot
  • Have the printer proof the colours before any print use
