# RehabMind: Logo Guidelines (Held R)

## 1. The logo
- **Idea**: an R with a break in its stem, where one dot holds the gap, like a word coming back.
- **Versions**: horizontal (primary), stacked, symbol only, wordmark only.
- **Files**: `master/` holds the source SVGs. `digital/` holds SVG and PNG exports, the colour PNGs and the web icon set.
- **Which file**: use `symbol-small` below 32 px. Its dot has wider gaps so it stays open.

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone of **1 × the dot's diameter** on all sides. It scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Stacked | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px (use `symbol-small`) | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour
| Name | Use | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Teal | R | #0F6E6E | 15, 110, 110 | 86, 0, 0, 57 |
| Deep teal | Wordmark | #0A3D3E | 10, 61, 62 | 84, 2, 0, 76 |
| Ember | Dot on light | #C8652B | 200, 101, 43 | 0, 50, 79, 22 |
| Sun | Dot on dark | #F4B860 | 244, 184, 96 | 0, 25, 61, 4 |

Teal and deep teal already exist in `DESIGN.md`. Ember and Sun are new. CMYK values are arithmetic conversions, not press-tested, and no Pantone match has been chosen: ask the printer to proof them.

**Approved pairs**: full colour on white or `#f2f5f5` · `on-dark` version on deep teal or teal (this is the app icon: white R, Sun dot, teal tile) · white on teal · black on white.
The dot alone is 3.9:1 on white, which is fine for a graphic part but too low for small text, so never set text in Ember.

## 5. Typography
- Wordmark: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next SemiBold, converted to outlines. It's the typeface the app already uses, designed for low-vision readers.
- Licence: SIL Open Font License, which allows logo use.
- Never retype the wordmark: use the supplied files.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't stretch, rotate or recolour outside the palette · don't add shadows, outlines or gradients · don't close the gap in the stem or remove the dot · don't change the lockup's spacing · don't place on busy photos without a solid container.
